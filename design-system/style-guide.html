Design System — v1.0

Structured, scaleable, editorial.

A design language built from the studio's own moodboards: espresso-dark backdrops, a single wine-red accent, and serif headlines that carry as much weight as the photography around them.

01 — Foundations

Ink and cream carry every layout. Wine and dusty blue are accents — use one per composition, rarely both at equal weight.
