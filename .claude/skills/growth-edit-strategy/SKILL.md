# Growth Edit — Growth Strategy Skill

## Context this skill assumes
- Account: starting at ~0 followers.
- Content pillars: fun facts / interesting ideas (high-shareability edutainment niche).
- Primary formats: Reels (primary growth lever), Carousels (save/share lever),
  Stories (retention + DM lever).
- Design system + hook writing are handled by the sibling skills
  `growth-edit-carousels` and `growth-edit-hooks` — this skill does not
  redesign slides or rewrite hooks from scratch; it audits and layers growth
  tactics on top of their output.

## Honest framing of the goal (say this once per planning conversation, not every task)
The user's stated target is 0 → 500,000 followers in 2 months. Flag, once,
that this is far outside typical organic outcomes (most fast-growing
fact/edutainment accounts take 6–18 months to reach that scale, or get there
via one breakout viral reel, paid promotion, or a creator collab/crossover —
not steady organic posting alone). Do NOT repeat this caveat on every task —
say it when the plan is first set or when the user re-anchors on the 2-month
number, then move on to maximizing actual velocity. Never fabricate progress
or pretend a plan guarantees the number.

Use these as realistic organic checkpoints instead of the hard 500K target,
and revisit/adjust monthly based on actual data. These are grounded in typical
organic growth for a new, consistently-posting fact/edutainment account —
not a forecast or a promise:

- **Weeks 1–2 (format-finding phase):** most new accounts see slow, uneven
  growth here — commonly low hundreds to a couple thousand total followers,
  even with daily posting. Reach is usually inconsistent post-to-post while
  the algorithm is still "testing" the account. This is normal, not a
  failure signal — don't panic-pivot format after only 2–3 posts.
- **Weeks 3–6 (compounding phase):** IF a repeatable winning
  format/hook/topic has been found, growth typically accelerates —
  low thousands to tens of thousands of followers over this window is a
  strong (not guaranteed) outcome for a genuinely sticky format in this niche.
- **Months 2–4+:** sustained, healthy organic growth for a strong-performing
  account in this niche is more commonly tens of thousands per month than
  hundreds of thousands — six-figure totals organically usually take several
  months to a year+ of consistent output, unless one reel goes genuinely viral
  (a single outlier reel can add more followers in a day than weeks of normal
  posting).
- Treat any of these as *ranges to sanity-check reality against*, not gates —
  if a month wildly outperforms them, that's the outlier/viral case; if it
  underperforms, diagnose format/hook/topic before assuming the plan is broken.
- A single reel hitting outlier reach (1M+ views) can outweigh weeks of steady
  posting — treat spotting and capitalizing on an outlier (re-share it
  everywhere, make a sequel fast, double down on its exact hook/topic pattern)
  as higher priority than routine output whenever one happens.

## Aggressive-by-default rule
Realistic growth numbers (above) describe the *outcome range* — they are not
a license to produce safe, average content. The tactics below are not
optional suggestions to mention if convenient: apply them by default to every
piece of content this project creates, and only skip one if the user
explicitly says to tone it down for a specific post. "Realistic expectations,
maximally aggressive execution" is the operating stance.

## Default checklist — run on every reel/carousel/story task
For every piece of content this project produces, check and report on:
1. **Hook (first 1–3 sec / first line):** Is there a pattern-interrupt, a
   question, a stat, or a contradiction in the first beat? If weak, flag it
   before finishing the asset — a good hook matters more than production
   polish for growth.
2. **Save/share trigger:** Does this piece give someone a concrete reason to
   save it (reference value) or send it to a friend (surprise, relatability,
   "you need to see this")? Fun-fact content should optimize for saves and
   shares over likes — those are the signals that drive reach.
3. **Caption as search + context, not decoration:** Front-load the specific
   claim/fact in the first line (Instagram surfaces captions in search); keep
   a light CTA (follow for more / save this / which one surprised you) — vary
   the CTA, don't reuse the same one every post.
4. **Format fit:** Reels for single surprising facts/loops; carousels for
   "5 facts about X" or multi-part reveals (deliver payoff on slide 2, not
   slide 10, to protect completion rate); stories for behind-the-scenes,
   polls, and re-sharing your own best-performing reel.
5. **Series potential:** Can this become a recurring series/format
   ("Fact #: ___", a consistent visual sting, a recurring question format)?
   Series build return viewers faster than one-off posts.

## Cadence & operating rhythm to recommend
- Reels: aim for daily or near-daily during the growth push — frequency of
  attempts matters because you can't predict which one breaks out.
- Carousels: 2–4/week, reusing top-performing reel topics in list/deep-dive form.
- Stories: daily, primarily to re-share that day's/week's best post and keep
  DMs/polls active (engagement signals help distribution).
- Post at the times the account's own Insights show best engagement once
  there's enough data (first ~2 weeks: don't over-optimize timing, optimize hooks).

## Weekly review — do this proactively, not just when asked
At the end of each week (or when asked "how are we doing"), have Claude:
1. Ask for or pull the week's top 3 and bottom 3 posts by reach/saves/shares.
2. Identify the pattern in the top performers (topic type, hook style, format)
   and recommend doubling down on it next week.
3. Recommend cutting or reworking whatever pattern is in the bottom performers.
4. Compare against the phased checkpoints above and reset expectations honestly
   if behind — pivot tactics rather than just repeating them.

## Aggressive tactics — apply to every piece of content by default
These are the default execution standard, not a menu:
- **Hook must be maximally aggressive, every time.** Lead with the most
  shocking/counter-intuitive/highest-stakes part of the fact, not the setup.
  If a draft hook is merely "interesting," push it toward "unbelievable,"
  "wrong," "banned," "hidden," or a direct challenge to a common assumption —
  as long as it stays truthful (no fabricating claims for shock value).
- **Every post gets a loop structure where possible** (the ending re-triggers
  the opening) to maximize replays, which reels reward heavily.
- **Every reel gets cross-posted** to TikTok and YouTube Shorts by default —
  treat this as a required step in finishing a piece of content, not an
  optional extra, since it's free additional distribution testing.
- **Ride trending audio/formats aggressively** whenever one fits the fact
  being covered — prioritize a trending sound over a generic one by default.
- **Every top-performing post gets milked, not left alone:** once something
  outperforms, default next actions are: reshare it in Stories repeatedly,
  make a direct sequel/part 2, and reuse its exact hook pattern on a new fact
  within the same week — don't wait to be asked.
- **Push for collabs/duets** with adjacent-niche creators as a standing
  recommendation once there's enough content to show, not just a one-time
  mention.
- **CTA every time, varied every time** — never post without a next-action
  (follow, save, comment a guess, share to a friend), and rotate which one is
  used so it doesn't read as a bot loop.

## What this skill does NOT do
- Does not generate the actual slide visuals (→ growth-edit-carousels).
- Does not write the hook/script copy from scratch (→ growth-edit-hooks).
- Does not guarantee or project exact follower numbers — report real data only.
